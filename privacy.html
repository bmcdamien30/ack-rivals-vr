ACK Rivals VR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

ACK Rivals VR ("the game") is made by GarnettWork. This policy explains what the game collects and what it doesn't.

What the game collects

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, voice, camera, location, contacts or payment details. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your progress and settings (for example your cash, chosen driver, graphics and music settings) are saved only on your headset, in the browser storage the game runs in. They never leave your device and we can't see them.

What the hosting service sees

The game loads from a website hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel automatically keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address, device type and time) to run and protect the service. We don't use these logs to identify you. Vercel's own privacy policy explains how it handles them.

Purchases

If you buy the game on the Meta Horizon Store, Meta handles the purchase. We don't receive your card details. Meta's privacy policy covers that data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your headset. To delete it, uninstall ACK Rivals VR, or clear its storage in the headset's app settings. If you have any question or want us to confirm there is nothing to delete, email us at bmcdamien30+garnettwork@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
